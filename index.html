Our Story

Rooted in a deep passion for authentic flavors and warm hospitality, 7 Star Indian Cuisine brings the rich culinary traditions of India to the heart of Bloomington.

A Local Passion

Founded by local restaurateurs Rajeev and Sunita Sharma, our journey began with a simple vision: to create a space where the Bloomington community could experience the true, uncompromised tastes of Northern India.

Drawing inspiration from their family's generations-old recipes, Rajeev and Sunita established 7 Star to celebrate the vibrant spices and complex aromas that define authentic Indian cooking. Whether you're craving a rich Chicken Korma, perfectly baked Garlic Naan from our tandoor, or a comforting Goat Karahi, every dish is crafted with care and tradition.

A Seat at Our Table

Located in the former Bedrak Cafe spot, our warm and inviting dining room has been completely transformed to offer an elegant yet comfortable atmosphere. We believe that great food is best enjoyed with great company, and our space is designed to bring people together.

From our daily lunch buffets to intimate dinners, we invite you to take a seat at our table and let us share our love for Indian cuisine with you. Welcome to 7 Star Indian Cuisine – where every meal is a celebration.
